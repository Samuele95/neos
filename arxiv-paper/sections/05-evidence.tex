\paragraph*{Session Dynamics.}

The session progressed through seven injection waves over 52~cycles.
The first wave (6~review patterns) formed the
\emph{defensive\_quality} attractor in only 3~cycles---coherence
jumped from $C = 0.42$ to $0.71$ from a uniformly constructive
resonance foundation (53\% strong, 47\% moderate, no inhibitors).
Subsequent waves expanded the field to 31~patterns by cycle~18
($C = 0.91$), triggering a \emph{mass saturation event} where seven
patterns crossed the activation ceiling simultaneously---a
phase transition driven by mutual resonance.

\paragraph*{SOLID Rediscovery.}

During Waves~2--3, an \emph{architecture quartet} emerged
\emph{before} the SOLID principles were explicitly injected---the
field rediscovered SOLID from first principles.  Once injected, each
SOLID principle paired with a GoF technique partner
(Table~\ref{tab:solid-resonance}), forming the \textbf{SOLID Decagon}
($\alpha_1$) with field constant $R_{\text{pt}} = 0.88$ and selection
function $r = 0.94$.  Wave~5 (OOP, p032--p040) confirmed the
rediscovery through three absorptions: SRP, ISP, and the Law of
Demeter were already present in the field.

\paragraph*{Convergence and Singleton.}

Ten GoF patterns (Wave~6) produced the ``Great Convergence''---14
patterns reaching ceiling in 10~cycles, with \texttt{@strategy} and
\texttt{@decorator} (the ``Golden Pair'') converging simultaneously,
each resonating with all five SOLID principles.  Wave~7 triggered
four self-referential absorptions.  Coherence reached 0.990.

The session's most dramatic event occurred at cycle~31: Singleton was
injected at 0.65---the lowest initial activation in field history---and
received zero strong resonances, a first.  Four lateral inhibitors
produced a net force of $-0.014$/cycle, causing the largest
single-injection coherence drop ($0.955 \to 0.941$).  Detailed
wave-by-wave dynamics appear in Appendix~\ref{app:sw-waves}.

\subsubsection{Key Dynamics}

Three phenomena distinguish this session:

\paragraph*{Rescue of a weak pattern.}
\texttt{@ip\_geolocalization} was injected at strength~0.50---the
lowest in the field and barely above threshold~$\tau = 0.40$.  Its
$R = 0.94$ bond with \texttt{@ip\_lookup} pulled it from near-expulsion
to activation~0.963 at equilibrium.  The field determined that
geographic context \emph{is} relevant to forensic analysis, even though
the human injector was uncertain.

\paragraph*{Hub migration.}
At cycle~3, \texttt{@protocol\_mapping} served as the field's hub
(activation~0.936).  By cycle~6, \texttt{@ip\_lookup} overtook it
(0.998), reorganising the attractor around the enrichment axis.  The
field concluded that \emph{understanding who is communicating} matters
more than \emph{how they communicate}---a structural insight that
mirrors standard DFIR methodology.

\paragraph*{Triple-hub convergence.}
By cycle~12, three independent analysis perspectives converged to
0.998: \texttt{@protocol\_mapping} (structural parsing),
\texttt{@ip\_lookup} (enrichment), and \texttt{@abuse\_ip} (threat
intelligence).  Convergent evidence from independent axes---protocol
structure \emph{says} something is wrong, IP enrichment \emph{confirms}
who is doing it, and threat intelligence \emph{classifies} what it
is---produced a deeply stable configuration with eigenvalue gap ratio
$\lambda_1/\lambda_2 = 8.52$.

\subsection{Limitations and Reproducibility}\label{sec:limitations}

The evidence now spans two sessions across two domains (abstract
reasoning and adversarial forensics), strengthening the domain-agility
claim but not eliminating key limitations: (i)~the eigenvector
structure discovered in the software-quality session may not generalise
to other abstract domains; (ii)~resonance strengths lack confidence
intervals or null-model comparison; (iii)~coherence curves
(Figures~\ref{fig:coherence-evolution} and~\ref{fig:pcap-coherence})
are based on engine telemetry, not independently measured;
(iv)~both sessions were conducted by the same operator on the same
substrate model, introducing potential operator and model bias.
Replication across additional domains, multiple operators, and diverse
substrate models is required before the field equation can be
considered validated.  The full session logs and specification files
are available at \url{https://github.com/samuelestronati/neos}.
